# Brief: Jazz Pharmaceuticals - board discussion materials

Audience: Jazz Pharmaceuticals board of directors (pre-read and live discussion).

Purpose: assess whether Jazz is undervalued relative to specialty-pharma peers and frame
strategic alternatives, including a potential sale at a control premium.

Must cover:
1. Executive summary with the key conclusions.
2. Company overview: business description and key statistics.
3. Historical financial summary (last three fiscal years): revenue, growth, EBITDA, margin,
   net income.
4. Trading comparables against specialty-pharma peers of similar scale.
5. Valuation summary (football field) across trading comps, precedent transactions,
   DCF and premiums paid, against the current share price.

Data: SEC 10-K XBRL for Jazz and peers; market data and valuation ranges from the
analyst's input sheet (inputs.xlsx). Public-company data only.
